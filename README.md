# Prototype 🚀
## Includes
- Playable Heroes
  - Pen Tester - Experts in exploiting the robot's AI slop to their advantage
  - Judge - Hits hard with his massive gavel and even harder with the law
  - Iron Lungs - Ol' Reliable portable base with resource extracting equipment
 - Enemy Robots
  - **FPV Drone** - Gives explosive hugs
  - **Spot** - Quadriped who axe you questions
  - **Terminator** - Biped who shoot first and ask prompts later
 - Enemy Buildings
  - 3D printer - Ctrl C, Ctrl V robots into existence
  - Enthenas - Allows for more combat ready enemies
  - Portable Server Rack (PSR) - The cyber brain of the operation
## Objective:
- Destroy or hack PSR
- Exfill with Iron Lungs
## Controls
- **WASD/Arrows =** Move camera
- **Scroll Wheel =** Zoom
- **Left Mouse Button =** Select unit or target
- **Right Mouse Button =** Move or perform action
- **Enter =** End Turn
- **Esc =** Pause Menu
